# Founder and decision ownership

## Implemented governance

Founder and Product Architect decide product behavior, priorities and product boundaries. Codex implements approved work and owns implementation quality, tests, refactoring and Git execution.

## Operating rule

Codex may clarify technical consequences, but does not introduce or redefine product behavior without approval. A task that changes product scope, verification semantics, public-profile behavior or trust rules requires an explicit product decision.

## Unresolved

- Named Product Architect and decision cadence.
- Approval process for product changes after Sprint 0.
- Ownership of legal, safety and launch-market decisions.
