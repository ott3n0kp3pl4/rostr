# Vision

Rostr is a trust layer for creator teams, starting in Telegram and the CIS market.

## Intended value

Career Passport is the planned professional identity: a portable public profile built from a verified career timeline. Talent and agency should both confirm employment history. Reputation should reflect verified events rather than star ratings.

## Strategic boundaries

- Rostr is not primarily a job board, LinkedIn clone or AI recruiting tool.
- Vacancies are postponed.
- Telegram is an initial distribution channel, not a permanent product boundary.
- The architecture must remain globally extensible.

## Current reality

Only onboarding is implemented. Career Passport, employment verification, reputation and public profiles remain unimplemented.

## Unresolved

- Which verified events form the first reputation model.
- How a talent or agency disputes employment history.
- The first non-Telegram product surface.
